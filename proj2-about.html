How We Grew

Our Journey

2019

The Spark

Started as a monthly meetup of 15 friends passionate about technology and entrepreneurship in a small coffee shop.

2020

Going Virtual

Pivoted to online events, growing our community to 100+ members across the country through virtual workshops and webinars.

2021

First Official Chapter

Launched our first official chapter with a dedicated co-working space, mentorship program, and structured weekly events.

2022

National Expansion

Expanded to 5 cities with the launch of regional chapters. Hosted our first Integrity Summit with 500+ attendees.

2024

The Launchpad Program

Introduced our startup incubator, helping 20 member-led ventures secure funding and mentorship from industry leaders.

2026

Today & Beyond

480+ members, 12 chapters, and a vibrant ecosystem of creators, builders, and innovators shaping the future together.
